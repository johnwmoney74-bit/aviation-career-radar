# Pilot source and KSU pathway audit

Research date: **October 17, 2026**. This is a starting source audit, not an exhaustive job-market search, recommendation to sign a program agreement, or verification of personal eligibility. Source pages can disagree or change. Application destinations and complete agreements control actual participation.

## Important findings for John

- Track program deadlines before CFI eligibility, but distinguish early engagement from an application to the full program.
- Program participation can constrain time building. JetBlue's published university framework specifically requires instruction at the partner school, which matters while waiting for KSU hiring.
- Company HQ does not establish where pilots live or report. NetJets and Flexjet publish residence/base policies separately.
- Public access in a browser is not the same as permitted automated collection. The tested Ohio University and Sinclair search pages returned HTML during research but their robots rules block the program's automatic checks. They remain useful manual sources and starter research leads.
- A pool posting, employer intake page, conditional program offer, and immediate job vacancy are different evidence types.

## Seven KSU-listed routes / relationships

KSU directory: https://www.kent.edu/cae/pilot-pathway-programs

The school page lists the seven names below. It does not establish seven currently accepting cadet programs. CommuteAir's entry is a relationship/career reference, not proof of a standalone student intake.

### Allegiant Accelerate

Official pilot page: https://www.allegiantair.jobs/careers/pilots/

The airline lists Kent State among its university partners and links a partner-student application. The same page contains a closed pathway-window notice and a heading accepting Accelerate cadets. Those competing signals mean **intake needs confirmation**, rather than a confident open/closed label. KSU describes university mentorship and conditional progression. Confirm current selection criteria, university obligations, financial terms and actual acceptance through KSU/current application.

### CommuteAir

Official resources: https://www.commuteair.com/resources/

KSU lists CommuteAir and the airline provides pilot/Aviate-related resources. This audit did not verify a current standalone KSU cadet application, financial benefit or student selection criteria. Treat it as an employer and relationship to investigate. Separate CommuteAir employment from entry into United Aviate; do not use older career-path articles as current transition promises.

### Delta Propel

Official criteria: https://www.delta.com/us/en/careers/pilots/propel

The current Collegiate & CFI route includes a qualifying AABI university program or affiliate-school background, active flight instruction and being within 12 months of ATP/R-ATP eligibility. It also lists medical, travel-document and work-authorization criteria. Kent State is an affiliate. Earlier-stage students can investigate the Propel Talent Community. These are different entry points. Review current Endeavor and Delta progression conditions; no mainline start date is guaranteed by this summary.

### JetBlue Gateway University

Official program: https://www.jetbluegateways.com/university-flight-ops

KSU is listed. Published entry criteria include partner-school status and at least an instrument rating (or instructor status), selection and employment eligibility. The framework includes graduation, **at least 500 hours of dual given at the partner school**, and further experience with a named partner or Part 121 regional. Current intake dates and the actual application requisition were not confirmed. Ask how inability to secure a KSU instructor position would affect completion before committing.

### Republic RJet Cadet

Official requisition: https://careers.rjet.com/jobs/jr-004825/rjet-cadet/

The published page separates R-ATP and unrestricted ATP routes, including different certificate requirements. It also specifies checkride, medical, work-eligibility and flight-hour/timing conditions. The wording of those limits should be clarified with recruiting rather than mechanically interpreted. Benefits include a conditional offer, recruiting contact and events. The cadet posting says no contractual obligation; that should not be generalized to a later employment/training agreement. A visible posting and Apply link do not prove an immediate training slot.

### Southwest Destination 225° University

Official program: https://careers.southwestair.com/us/en/D225University

The university page states applications are currently closed and offers a Talent Community. It lists KSU. Requirements include partner-school enrollment, completed freshman year/private training requirements and other selection conditions. It describes a university instructor commitment and says most flight partners require two years, with partner-dependent terms. John reported denial at the final interview; the exact pathway and official reapplication waiting period were not independently verified. Keep the decision email for that detail. The program is not a guaranteed Southwest job.

### United Aviate University

Official route: https://unitedaviate.com/aviate-program-career-paths/attending-a-university/

KSU lists Aviate, but the airline detail page returned HTTP 403 during this audit. Do not infer current personal eligibility, intake, allowed employers or transition hours from search snippets or old material. Manually review the current university/network pages and latest Aviate Program Guide, then confirm KSU-specific participation. The dashboard labels this uncertainty explicitly.

## Pilot job-source strategy

1. **Direct employer pages:** Primary evidence for current qualifications, job location and application destination. Maintain an employer watchlist even when no job is advertised.
2. **JSfirm:** https://www.jsfirm.com/ — broad aviation discovery; automatic integration not established here.
3. **Airline Apps:** https://www.airlineapps.com/ — participating employer applications and public recruiting information. Private applications and account changes remain manual.
4. **Handshake:** https://support.joinhandshake.com/hc/en-us/articles/218693388-Saving-Job-Searches-and-Receiving-Job-Alerts — saved searches and alerts are available. Its terms prohibit bulk scraping: https://joinhandshake.com/legal/tos/. Email import is deferred by John's preference.
5. **Directories:** https://www.aopa.org/destinations/ — useful for discovering schools, not evidence that a school is hiring.
6. **Recruiting events:** https://www.fapa.aero/events — inspect dates, attendees and experience requirements before planning travel.
7. **Optional paid boards:** https://ppb.climbto350.com/ and https://bizjetjobs.com/ — evaluate added value against free coverage before subscribing. Login/payment does not establish automation permission. No paid integration is included.
8. **Context sources:** Airline Pilot Central can help direct research, but date-check figures against employers/contracts. Forums and word of mouth stay unverified leads until corroborated.

## Technical evidence and limits

Greenhouse documents public read endpoints: https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html. Lever documents its public postings API: https://github.com/lever/postings-api. API support is an adapter option for employers using those systems, not universal aviation coverage.

The live prototype checks robots before page/API reads and does not crawl whole sites, log into boards or evade blocks. A fetch success means a page was retrieved, not that all listings were captured. Source results and collection counts from the completed live test are recorded separately in `research/source-test.json`.

News/event starter pages: KSU Aeronautics https://www.kent.edu/aeronautics ; FAA https://www.faa.gov/newsroom ; FAPA https://www.fapa.aero/events. These produce page-change notices, not automatically verified news claims. Source content changes do not overwrite manually researched program facts.
